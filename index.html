TPMidi Config
Connect TPMidi to begin.

Map the surface
before the DAW.

Select a DAW profile, assign three slider CCs and define one rotary target and press action for every encoder. Clockwise always increases; counter-clockwise always decreases.

Controller surface

Select a control to jump to its mapping. Labels update from the current configuration.

Control editor

Choose a profile, then select a control on the surface to edit it.

Firmware update

Connect TPMidi A/B to check firmware.

Factory reset

Erase saved mappings and restore the device defaults.
